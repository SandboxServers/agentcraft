## The rule (coordinator's decision under D-MP05)

A position is protected when it is **inside a plot or face-adjacent to a position inside a plot** — a one-block margin around the plot's box. One lookup implements it (`protectedPlotAt` for the plot, `foreignPlotAt` for the plot the player may not edit) and every position a handler tests goes through it: both break callbacks (the block being broken), the use path (clicked block, clicked-face neighbour and a `BlockItem`'s resolved destination), the bucket path (hit block and the block the fluid would go into) and `explosionProtects`. The owner and gamemasters may edit the margin of their plot as they may edit the plot. The margin closes the boundary hole: a block just outside (a bed foot, a wall holding a torch, sand's support, a door's lower half) can no longer be broken or blown up to remove its protected partner.

For a player who may not edit the plot (`active` and a foreign plot):

- **Non-empty hand:** always refused. `action=place` when the held item is a `BlockItem` (including `SolidBucketItem`), otherwise `action=use`. This covers every block-changing item, not only `BlockItem` and buckets: flint and steel, bone meal, axe, hoe, shovel, spawn egg, end crystal, brush, dye and the rest. The non-empty hand is refused even on the walk-in blocks: an iron door passes the click on to the held item, which would then be placed inside the plot.
- **Empty hand:** allowed only on a `DoorBlock`, `TrapDoorBlock`, `FenceGateBlock`, `ButtonBlock` or `LeverBlock`, so a visitor can walk in. Every other block is refused with `action=use`, because each of them changes state on an empty-hand click (cake, note blocks, repeaters and comparators, redstone wire, flower pots, dragon eggs, candles, sweet berry bushes and cave vines, composters, respawn anchors, containers, signs, jukeboxes, lecterns, decorated pots, ...).
- **Placement (non-empty hand):** refused when the clicked block, the clicked-face neighbour, or the `BlockItem`'s real destination (resolved through `BlockItem.updatePlacementContext`) is protected. The destination check keeps the margin, so a bed whose head would land inside (destination in the margin), a door or double-plant top written at `pos.above()` and a scaffolding row's real destination are all refused. The only vanilla item that places further away than the clicked-face neighbour is scaffolding (`ScaffoldingBlockItem.updatePlacementContext`); resolving the context covers it.
- **An eye of ender on an end portal frame:** refused when any position in the 7x7 square at the frame's height (three blocks each way, the largest distance between a frame block and a cell of the portal it encloses) is protected. The frame and the clicked face can both be outside the margin while the portal vanilla creates reaches inside, so the margin alone does not cover this action.
- **Telemetry position:** the refusal carries the offending position, not necessarily the clicked one, so a placement from outside that would land in the margin logs the margin destination.
- **Buckets** are checked twice: `UseBlockCallback` (the `useItemOn` path) with the clicked/adjacent positions, and `UseItemCallback` (the `useItem` path) with the same ray as `BucketItem.use` — the item's own fluid context (`ClipContext.Fluid.SOURCE_ONLY` for an empty bucket, `NONE` for a filled one) and reach. The hit block and the block the fluid would go into are both checked, so water in front of a plot cannot hide a block inside it.
- **Place-on-water items** (`PlaceOnWaterBlockItem`: lily pad, frogspawn) return `PASS` from `useOn` and place from `use()` at the hit position's `above()`. `UseItemCallback` now runs the same `SOURCE_ONLY` ray as the item, checks the hit block and the block above it, and refuses with `action=place` at the position that would change.
- **AgentCraft stations** are handled client-side and return `FAIL` there, so their clicks never reach the server and need no special case (audit A-11).

### Items that place or change a block from `use()`

The callers of `getPlayerPOVHitResult` are `BottleItem`, `BucketItem`, `BoatItem`, `SpawnEggItem`, `PlaceOnWaterBlockItem` and `EnderEyeItem`. Only two change a block through `use()`:

| Item | `use()` effect | Covered by |
|---|---|---|
| `PlaceOnWaterBlockItem` (lily pad, frogspawn) | places the block at the water hit's `above()` | `UseItemCallback` (this round) |
| `BucketItem` | places or removes a fluid | `UseItemCallback` |
| `BottleItem` | fills a glass bottle from a water source; only the held item changes | no block change |
| `BoatItem` | spawns a boat entity | no block change |
| `SpawnEggItem` | spawns a mob entity on a liquid | no block change |
| `EnderEyeItem` | `use()` throws an eye-of-ender entity; the block-affecting path is `useOn` on an end portal frame, which replaces the frame's 3x3 interior with portal blocks, a cell up to three blocks from the clicked frame | `UseBlockCallback`: the non-empty-hand rule **plus** a 7x7 scan at the frame's height, because the margin alone does not cover the portal the eye completes |

## APIs and implementation notes for later packets

- `PlotProtectionFeature.explosionProtects(ServerLevel level, BlockPos pos)` is public and static. Returns true when multiplayer is active and the position is inside any plot or one block around it (the margin). Any mixin or feature that must skip block destruction inside protected plots can call it.
- `RefusalThrottle` is public: `new RefusalThrottle(LongSupplier)`, `shouldLog(UUID, String action, int plot)`, `forget(UUID)`, `clear()`. The refusal itself never depends on the log: `refuse` denies first and consults the throttle only to decide whether to emit telemetry.
- The gate reads `MpServerConfig.current()` on every event, never caches it in `init()`.
- **Break injection.** `PlayerBlockBreakEvents.BEFORE` is wired by Fabric API 0.161.0 (`fabric-events-interaction-v0` 5.3.6) in `ServerPlayerGameModeMixin` with `@Inject(at = @At(value = "INVOKE", target = "Lnet/minecraft/world/level/block/Block;playerWillDestroy(...)"), method = "destroyBlock")` — i.e. at the call of `Block.playerWillDestroy` inside `ServerPlayerGameMode.destroyBlock`, not at HEAD, so the level's `removeBlock` has not run yet when the event fires. `AttackBlockCallback` is injected by the same mixin at HEAD of `handleBlockBreakAction`. Both signatures were read from the jar with `javap -p -v`.
- **Op check.** `mayEdit` uses `player.permissions().hasPermission(Permissions.COMMANDS_GAMEMASTER)`. `PlayerList.isOp` is not used: it is true for an op-list entry at any level, and `GameTestServer.operatorUserPermissions()` is `ALL` (permission level 0), so an `isOp` check would let every op through.
- **The gate is true on the game-test server.** Vanilla `GameTestServer.isDedicatedServer()` returns false; Fabric's game-test API (`GameTestServerMixin` in `fabric-gametest-api-v1`) overrides it to `true`. Multiplayer itself is still off there by default (no config file), so every test installs `MpServerConfig` with `MpServerConfig.install` and restores it in `finally`.
- Item use is guarded through `UseBlockCallback` (the `useItemOn` path) and `UseItemCallback` (the `useItem` path). The `ItemEvents.USE_ON` event was not used: its `@WrapOperation`-based injection in `ItemStackMixin` proved unreliable with game-test mock players, and the equivalent decisions live in `UseBlockCallback`.

## Known limits

- **Structures completed from outside the margin (coordinator's scope decision).** The eye of ender is one member of a class of actions where a player outside the margin completes a structure whose body reaches into the plot: lighting a nether portal whose obsidian frame crosses the edge, and completing a golem or wither pattern whose body is partly inside. Every member needs blocks the owner placed across their own plot's edge, because a visitor cannot place in the plot or its margin. This packet closes only the eye of ender, because it replaces existing blocks; lighting a nether portal, a golem and a wither are listed for the owner's decision and are not closed here.
- One block of margin does not cover a chain of supported blocks longer than one block that starts outside the margin: a bed whose foot is two blocks out and whose head lands in the margin (`AbstractBedBlock.setPlacedBy` writes `pos.relative(FACING)`), a scaffolding row that starts more than one block out, or any similar support chain. The margin refuses the mutation when the destination itself touches the plot, not when a second half reaches one block further.
- A visitor holding any item cannot open a door inside a plot, because the non-empty-hand rule refuses every block change and there is no vanilla signal that separates "open the door" from "use the item".
- Water or lava poured outside a plot flows in, and fire lit outside spreads in: the flow and the spread change blocks inside the plot without a player event whose position is the inside block. (Only the player's own right-click is gated.)
- A visitor's projectile can change blocks inside a plot: a flaming arrow primes TNT and an arrow breaks a decorated pot. `Projectile.mayInteract` checks spawn protection only, Fabric has no projectile event, and closing this would need a second mixin, which the packet's Decisions do not allow.
- Trampling (farmland, turtle eggs) changes a block inside a plot and is not a player event the packet can gate.
- Pistons and dispensers are outside the packet: they change blocks on their own schedule, not through a player event.
- Plots are compared by coordinates, without the dimension: `Plots.directory().plotAt(pos)` has no level argument.

The whole-line tests assert the exact `event=plot_edit_refused player=<uuid> studio=<uuid> plot=1 action=break|place|use pos=x,y,z` string from `MpLog.capture()`, so they prove all five fields for one refusal of each action. `explosionKeepsProtectedBlock` places a breakable block inside the plot (x=46), one in the margin (x=47) and one two blocks outside (x=48), all within a power-4 blast; the inside and margin blocks survive and the outside block is destroyed. `boundaryBedSurvivesVisitorBreak` has the owner place a bed through the real path with its foot in the margin and its head inside, then a visitor's break of the foot is refused and both halves remain; `boundaryBedSurvivesExplosion` explodes beside the foot and both halves remain. `visitorEnderEyeRefusedNearPlot` places a lone end portal frame three blocks outside the -z edge: the frame and the clicked face are outside the margin, but the 7x7 square at the frame's height reaches the plot at z=-36, so the eye is refused with the frame's position; five blocks out the same square stops outside the margin and the eye is inserted, and the owner may insert at three. The fixture remembers the item entities already present in the plot's box grown by 16 blocks when it is created; `close()` empties every jukebox it placed, restores the saved blocks, and then removes every item entity in that box that was not there at the start, so restoration-generated drops (an unsupported lily pad, a bed's orphaned half, a jukebox disc) do not leak into the shared world. The fire test uses a 7x7 floor inside the plot and a `KEEP`+fire explosion; about 49 air positions are candidates, so without the `createFire` filter the test fails with near certainty.
